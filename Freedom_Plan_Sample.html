Part A · 03

Key observations

How customer service runs today, and why this role exists.

Harbourline Supplies sells packaging and consumables to cafés and restaurants, with about 120 active customers. Customer service and sales already happen, but they’re spread across a small team who all juggle other work, across email, phone, website chat and WhatsApp.

What we heard

Repeat orders are entered by hand, a handful of credit customers need regular follow-up, and around 400 past customers haven’t ordered in a while.

Hiring insight

Jordan wants one accountable owner: someone careful and warm who catches the details, works through the list without chasing, and is in place before the busy season.
