Key observations

How content runs today, and why this role exists.

The Bigger Stage helps founders and business leaders build authority, visibility and trust through compelling stories and powerful communication. The business is entering a growth phase, and Matt now creates more media than one person can repurpose.

What we heard

A daily 10-minute audio podcast (Mon–Thu), a video interview podcast with 30+ full episodes on YouTube, client interviews to repurpose, and a monthly newsletter built with Claude. Editing happens in Descript.

Hiring insight

Matt doesn’t need a scheduler. Matt needs a skilled, curious creative collaborator who already knows Descript and AI tools, brings ideas, and doesn’t wait for exact instructions.

Toolkit · 02

Working with Matt

Fill this in together in Week 1. It becomes your Specialist’s guide to Matt.

How Matt works

Records, hands off the files, and wants them handled. Pitch ideas, try things, and report back on what worked. Slack (or WhatsApp) for day-to-day.

What matters most

Social media first: clips that feel native to each platform, polished English and care for the details. Calculated risks are welcome.

Repeat it back

Before starting a new task, repeat the instructions back to Matt in your own words, so you both get it right the first time.
