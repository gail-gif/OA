Key observations

How the farm’s admin runs today, and why this role exists.

Conscious Ground is a regenerative family farm in the Byron Shire, with wholesale and market produce, an in-house kitchen, events and a growing team. Your Australian admin has moved on, and the admin now runs across Xero, Employment Hero, around 40 Slack channels, Mailchimp and four inboxes.

What we heard

Monday to Wednesday are the busy days, supporting harvesters and customer orders. Invoices and picklists go out by 2pm Tuesday, timesheets are checked weekly, and organic certification logs must stay audit-ready.

Hiring insight

You already work with an exceptional marketing VA and want an admin specialist at the same level: self-managing, warm, and someone who checks their own work and never repeats a mistake.

Working with May

Fill this in together in Week 1. It becomes your Coordinator’s guide to you.

How May works

Shows how it’s done, then asks you to show it back: send a screenshot when a task is done. Urgent items go to the Admin Private Slack channel, not just email.

What matters most

Accuracy first: double-check every number, date and bank detail. Warm, honest, never stiff. Mistakes are fine; repeating them isn’t.

Her busiest times

Monday to Wednesday, supporting harvesters and orders. Invoices and picklists are due by 2pm Tuesday. Thursday and Friday are lighter.
