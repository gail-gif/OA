Part A · 01

Key Observations

Hanks Tea supplies wholesale materials to cafés, bubble tea stores and other food and beverage businesses, with about 100–150 active customers. Customer service and sales already happen, but they’re spread across a small team who all juggle other work. As the business grows, Hank wants one accountable owner.

What we heard

Enquiries arrive across seven channels. Repeat orders are entered by hand. About 10 credit customers need AR follow-up, and 500–600 dormant customers sit untouched.

Hiring insight

Hank manages the VA directly. He wants someone careful and patient who catches the details, in place by November ahead of the Christmas rush.

Part A · 04 · Design

The Role We Recommend

A Customer Service & Sales Support VA who owns six areas, so every customer has one point of contact.

Customer Enquiries

Answering every enquiry within two hours, across WhatsApp, WeChat, email and more.

Order Taking & Entry

Delivery & Escalation

Chasing the right team member, then updating the customer and closing the loop.

Following up open orders, and overdue accounts three days after they fall due.

Customer Reactivation

Re-engaging dormant customers, aiming for 15 back ordering each month.

Tracking & Reporting

A simple customer tracker, plus a weekly enquiry, AR and reactivation report.
