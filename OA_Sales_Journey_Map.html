How we explain it

Explaining the Freedom Plan to a lead

This is how the Freedom Plan, the 5D system and the price come across on our calls today.

The 30-second version

"For a $500 refundable deposit, we start your search. We map exactly what to hand off, build your Freedom Plan, and present you 3–5 hand-picked candidates within 1–2 weeks. The $500 comes off your package when you go ahead."

We describe the plan to clients as "the Bible for you and your Angel": KPIs, success metrics, a not-to-do list, training guides and a personal operating manual.

The lines we lean on

  • "We're not the typical BPO. Our motto is to create freedom for our clients."
  • "You're not just hiring one person, you're hiring the whole Client & Angel Experience team."
  • "We only hire the top 1%, all with 3+ years' experience. You only teach them your process, not the skills."
  • Your Angel becomes "your number two, your accountability partner." Say Executive Assistant or Specialist, not "VA".

The 5D Freedom Staffing System™ is the structure of every Freedom Plan

DDesign

Map the bottlenecks: where the owner's time goes today.

DDefine

Set the success metrics and KPIs for the role.

DDiscover

Find the right hire: skills, experience and tools.

DDeploy

A 1–90 day onboarding plan: shadow, then run, then own.

DDevelop

CAX, Team Leader, training and coaching for the long run.

What's inside the plan

Part A: Our Recommendation. Used once, to decide: key observations, the leverage gap, their Freedom Stage, the role, candidate profiles, the investment and next steps.

Part B: The Freedom Playbook. Used for the whole relationship: a first-time starter pack, the 12-month journey, the first 90 days, the Freedom Scorecard, KPIs, a delegation list, an SOP library, a wins log and the CAX framework.

When they say no

Lost leads are paused, not finished

Our Q2 review found that most lost deals come down to timing and comparison, not OA's quality. The three main reasons are: still comparing, not ready yet, and still shopping around.

Step 1: score every Freedom Audit with P.A.I.N. (out of 8)

7–8Freedom Plan ready. Send the proposal and push for the deposit.
5–6Qualified. Send the proposal and follow up.
3–4Nurture. Stay in touch until the timing is right.
0–2Disqualified. Log the reason and close.

Step 2: answer the objection

"We're weighing it up" / "We're talking to others"Answer: lead with outcomes and value, and present the $500 Freedom Plan as a low-commitment first step that's refundable before interviews.
"We're not ready to hire yet"Answer: send the proposal "for future reference", note their target start date, and nurture. Don't pressure a lead who has said no.
"It's more than we expected"Answer: show that the price is all-in, sell the whole team rather than one person, apply the EO or referral 10%, step down to Angel Essentials, or re-scope the role.
"We had a bad VA before" / "3 months is a big commitment"Answer: point to Team Leader oversight, EOD reports and free replacement. Ask "What would make you confident?" and send sample profiles.
"I only need 5 hours a week"Answer: hold the 20-hour minimum and frame it as a growth plan. Send the proposal and profiles anyway.
"Is our data safe?" / "Will they understand our market?"Answer: a virtual desktop, a password manager (LastPass or Keeper), insurance, and Angels with Australian market experience. The Freedom Plan sets the brief.

Step 3: if it's still a no, this is the lost-lead loop

1 · Record

Move the opportunity to Closed Lost in GoHighLevel and record the lost reason and lead source.

2 · Nurture

Send value emails at Day 30, 60 and 90. Workshop invites and lead magnets keep OA front of mind.

3 · Reactivate

Daily BDM: 20 leads, 20 emails. The five touches are the Bottleneck quiz, a call, a "tasks you can outsource" email, an AI and systems angle, and a downsell to Ms Linh's programs.

4 · Back to the start

Offer a new 20-minute Freedom Audit. This is how 2 lost clients were won back in Q2.

Glossary

Angel
Our name for the offshore professional (usually in the Philippines) placed with a client. Also called an Associate, EA or Specialist; we avoid "VA" on calls.
Freedom Audit
The free first call, also called the discovery call. We learn the business, qualify the lead and explain how OA works.
Freedom Plan / Freedom Plan fee
The client's personalised hiring and onboarding plan. It's paid for by the $500 refundable deposit, which is credited to their package.
Freedom Playbook
Part B of the Freedom Plan: the living document used at every review for 12 months.
P.A.I.N.
Our qualification score: Problem, Aligned need, Investment readiness, Now. Each scores 0–2, for a total out of 8.
5D Freedom Staffing System™
Design, Define, Discover, Deploy, Develop: our method and the structure of every Freedom Plan.
Freedom Stages
Task Delegator → Process Builder → Outcome Owner → Operator → Freedom: how far the owner has stepped back.
Alignment Call
The call after the deposit where we define the role, KPIs and brief for Recruitment.
Strategy Session
The call where we present the Freedom Plan and 3–5 candidate profiles.
DDR
The onboarding and billing record completed when a client hires. It triggers invoicing, the contract and Angel onboarding.
CAX
Client & Angel Experience: the team that looks after clients and Angels after the sale.
EOD report
The end-of-day report every Angel sends the client: done, in progress, questions, tomorrow.
BDM
Business development: our daily outreach to past and lost leads (20 leads, 20 emails).
GHL
GoHighLevel, our main CRM and automation platform.
EO
Entrepreneurs' Organization. Members are a key referral source and get 10% off the agency fee.
Angel Happiness Lifetime Guarantee
A free replacement Angel at any time, with a 1-week free trial before billing resumes.
