Part A · 01

Key Observations

Spinifex Energy is a Queensland energy brokerage and solar business run by a team of three. Tom’s Brisbane admin is on maternity leave and his part-time offshore data person struggled with power cuts, so he wants one full-time person to cover both roles.

What we heard

SME work is high volume but simple: bill, comparison report, contract, Xero invoice. C&I tenders need data from retailers. Accuracy is critical on large accounts.

Hiring insight

His last hire lacked the confidence to learn and checked everything with him. Tom wants a fast learner who acts, uses the SOPs and owns mistakes.

Part B · 06 · Define

Success Metrics

1 Speed to client

Reports the same day a bill arrives; LOAs and contracts out same day.

Ask: “Which bills or contracts are still waiting?”

2 Data accuracy

Zero errors in NMI, rate or term fields; no duplicate records.

Ask: “What did you check before sending this?”

3 Pipeline follow-through

Every live lead has a next step; none stalled over three days.

Ask: “Which deals haven’t moved this week?”

4 C&I & solar delivery

Tenders out once all data is in; deliveries confirmed before installs.

Ask: “What’s due for the next tender or install?”
