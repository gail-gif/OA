Part A · 01

Key Observations

Conscious Ground is a regenerative family farm in the Byron Shire: wholesale and market produce, a kitchen, events and a growing team. May’s Australian admin has moved on, and the admin runs across Xero, Employment Hero, around 40 Slack channels, Mailchimp and four inboxes.

What we heard

Mon–Wed are the busy days, supporting harvesters and orders. Invoices and picklists go out by 2pm Tuesday, timesheets are checked weekly, and organic certification logs stay audit-ready.

Hiring insight

May already has an exceptional marketing VA and wants an admin specialist at the same level: self-managing, warm, and someone who checks their own work and never repeats a mistake.

Part A · 06 · Safeguards

Payroll & Sensitive Information

Yes, your Coordinator can handle payroll, timesheets and employment contracts, with clear access and approvals.

What they handle

Timesheet checks and submission, payroll preparation, employment contracts, onboarding, offboarding and HR records.

Access & security

Logins and card details shared through Keeper, never by email or chat. A work email and view-only bank access.

Approvals & payments

The Coordinator prepares payments and payroll; May approves and releases. Agreed limits for small online purchases.

Confidentiality

Signed NDA, personal and business records kept separate, and indemnity cover through Outsourcing Angel.

Part B · 06 · Define

Success Metrics

1 Order cycle on time

Invoices and picklists out by 2pm Tuesday; customers called straight away if an order can’t be filled.

Ask: “Which orders are still waiting?”

2 Accuracy & self-checking

Every number and price double-checked; no repeated errors; mistakes self-reported with a fix.

Ask: “What did you check before sending this?”

3 Accounts & timesheets

Timesheets submitted on time; overdue accounts followed up by phone.

Ask: “Which accounts are overdue this week?”

4 Compliance & readiness

Renewals flagged weeks ahead; certification logs and audit evidence always current.

Ask: “What’s coming up in the next 60 days?”
