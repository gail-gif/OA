Part A · 03

Key observations

How the business runs today, and why this role exists.

Harbourline Supplies sells packaging and consumables to cafés and restaurants, with about 120 active customers. Customer service and sales already happen, but they’re spread across a small team who all juggle other work. As the business grows, Jordan wants one accountable owner.

What we heard

Enquiries arrive by email, phone, the website chat and WhatsApp. Repeat orders are entered by hand. A handful of credit customers need follow-up, and around 400 past customers haven’t ordered in a while.

Hiring insight

Jordan manages the VA directly and wants someone careful and patient who catches the details, including what the customer actually needs, in place before the busy season.
