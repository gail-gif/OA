# Reply / follow-up email (one person)

Read the `oa-basics` skill first. This is Gail's most frequent task (dozens a day).

## Steps
1. **Find the right thread.** Search Gmail by name, email or company. If there are two people with the same first name (e.g. Jason Tran vs Jason at HCHC), ask, or pick the one that matches the context and say which.
2. **Read the whole thread** plus any Fathom call with them, so the reply fits what was said.
3. **Draft in the thread**, keeping the same To/CC. Only change CCs if Gail says so (e.g. "without CC'ing Ms Linh").
4. Show Gail the text and say it's in Drafts. Send only if she says so.

## How to write it
- Warm, polite, short. "Not pushy but eager." Encourage questions: "No question is too small."
- Already spoke to them? No introduction.
- **Booking**: include the Calendly Freedom Audit link and offer flexibility.
- **Proposal follow-up**: check in gently and offer to answer questions or adjust.
- **"Not now"**: thank them, leave the door open, offer to reconnect later. Offer a calendar reminder (e.g. "Reach out to Kirsty", 1 Dec).
- **Declined / went elsewhere**: gracious close-out ("my door is always open"). Optionally ask, gently, why.
- **Deposit / payment**: AUD or USD deposit link. Explain options plainly (card or AU BECS direct debit; no card surcharge under AU law).
- **Can't help** (e.g. a role we can't fill): say so kindly, suggest they hire locally, and leave room for other services.
- **Promos**: only offers Gail has approved for that client. Never stack discounts.
- **Time zones**: give both their time and PHT if a meeting time is involved (see `meeting-booking`).
