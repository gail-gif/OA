## Agreed framework (as of Oct 2026)
- OA contracts the client; the partner contracts OA. OA invoices the client and the partner invoices OA.
- **OA keeps 15%** of the client price; the partner gets 85%. Example: $9,000 → OA $1,350 / partner $7,650. Referral-only model (10% vs 15%) is still **undecided**.
- 3-month commitment, then month to month with 30 days' notice. Monthly billing, or 3 months upfront at 5% off (the partner must agree).
- Partners are paid monthly, after a review confirms the client is happy.
- The partner gives a **detailed, itemised monthly report** of deliverables (not a summary). This is how OA checks quality, since no one at OA can QA the work.
- Sales stays the client's contact before and after kick-off. Sales, Ms Linh and Ms Wendy are CC'd on all client emails.
- Every proposal shows pricing options per package, discussed at the Alignment session.

## Client pricing slides
- Show **one all-in price** ("$X a month + GST, billed monthly by Outsourcing Angel"), the total incl. GST and the team size covered.
- **Never** show the OA/partner split to the client.
- Include "OA is with you the whole way" (billing, monthly check-ins) and the getting-started steps: go-ahead → invoice month 1 → kickoff after payment.
- Make it look like OA's, not a copy of the partner's slide.
- Linh prefers presenting it in a meeting rather than just sending it.
