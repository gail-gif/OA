# Monday Weekly Huddle

Read the `oa-basics` skill first.

Database: Notion "☀️ Weekly Huddle" (`collection://525fd8ed-48b2-4a33-8f91-5b2d597d1278`). One row per person per week. Fields: Name, Priorities, Achievements, Challenges, One Word, Most Important Learning, Rate and Why, Date.
Find (or create) **Gail's row for this week**. Only touch the field she asks for. Fill fields one at a time.

## 1. Achievements
Data: Google Calendar for last Mon–Sun (skip Focus time, cancelled and declined), plus Gmail **sent** for the same dates. Leave bulk campaigns out of the line-by-line list and count them separately.

Always start with the **standing 10**, in this order:
1. Sales end of week reporting
2. Trifecta meeting
3. Notion (sales calendar update)
4. GHL update
5. Council meeting
6. Recruitment and sales collaboration – interview scheduling, candidate discussion
7. Meta ad leads that were on VM – call, email and SMS
8. BDM – call, email and follow-up
9. Tasks follow-up – old leads
10. Email all leads forwarded by Ms Linh, Scott and Yuki to invite them to a Freedom Audit

Then add: Daily quick updates to Ms Linh • Daily follow-ups on open leads and proposals • Sales dashboard – still being refined • plus one short line per real event, grouped where helpful (WIN, Freedom Audits, Alignment calls, Freedom Plans created and sent, Strategy sessions, Partnership emails, Follow-ups). Format: `◦ Client (Company) – what happened (day)`.
Leave out test bookings and say so. "Do not make it complicated": simple bullets.

## 2. Priorities
Start with the fixed **Daily** list:
- Quick update to Ms Linh
- Follow up on open leads and proposals
- Huddle sheet
- Check emails and GHL for new leads
- Call, email and SMS new and VM leads
- Coordinate with other teams regarding sales updates that will impact other teams

Then "This week" from her calendar, in PHT with day and time: Freedom Audit • Alignment call • Strategy session • Freedom Plan creation • BDM and follow-up.

## 3. Rate and Why
About 3 short sentences on the work week, then `Personal: …` with the highlights she gives you. **Never change the star rating.** Shorten if she asks.

## 4. Most Important Learning
`Work: …` and `Personal: …`. Short, or a single sentence if she asks. Don't overstate (an unsigned deal is a "new client", not a "signed client").

## 5. Challenges
Short. Lead with what she names (e.g. fewer inbound leads). Leave out anything she removes (e.g. RDO).

## 6. One Word
Suggest 2–3 options (e.g. "Momentum").

## 7. Highlight / Heads up / Help (for the huddle or L10)
Take these from the latest weekly sales report:
- **Highlight**: deposits, partner wins, milestones.
- **Heads up**: risks and patterns (low inbound, objections, no-shows, payment-before-start pushback).
- **Help**: per team (Marketing, Recruitment/Charyl, Accounts, Leadership).
She adds her own, then "combine all". Ask before writing these into Notion.

Note: an attempted Friday automation ("Gail weekly huddle achievements") had no connectors attached, so it can't read Calendar or Notion. Run this skill by hand on Mondays instead.
