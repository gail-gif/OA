# Candidate profiles and Freedom Plan email

Read `.claude/skills/oa-basics/SKILL.md` first.

## Steps
1. Pull context: the latest Fathom call with the client (Alignment or Strategy), and the recent email thread.
2. Use the candidate profiles from Recruitment (Charyl) **word for word**: name heading, short paragraph, then a "<Name>'s Profile" link (Google Slides). Keep them in the order Gail pasted them, and check each link matches its name.
3. Write it warmly:
   - Open personally, mentioning the call ("It was lovely chatting with you again!") and something specific about their business.
   - **No introduction** of Gail or OA, and no recap of requirements they already told you.
   - "As promised, here are the candidates I mentioned…"
   - If the Freedom Plan is included, say it's attached and explain in 2–3 lines how to use it (Part A to decide, Part B to keep using with their Angel). Say Gail is their account manager if that's the case.
   - Ask which candidates they'd love to meet and offer to set up interviews around their schedule.
   - Close "Looking forward to hearing your thoughts!" then "Warmly, Gail" plus her sign-off.
4. Subject suggestions: "Your Freedom Plan and <Role> Candidate Profiles", "Your shortlisted candidates for <Company>", "Your Freedom Plan for <Company>".
5. Save as a **draft**. Remind Gail to **attach the Freedom Plan** file (the Gmail tool can't attach).
