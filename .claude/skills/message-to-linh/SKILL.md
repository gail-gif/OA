# Messages to Ms Linh / Ms Wendy

Read the `oa-basics` skill first.

- **WhatsApp means chat text only.** Don't create an email draft. Give the message in chat for Gail to copy.
- Style: short, polite and respectful ("Ms Linh", never "Lyn"). One point per message. A 🙏 and "would love to hear your thoughts" fit her tone.
- Updates: what happened → what it means → what Gail needs (a decision, approval or input).
- "Just sharing this to add to your thoughts" works for FYI notes.
- **Review-request emails** (framework or proposal docs): reply in the existing thread when there is one (e.g. Ms Wendy's doc thread). Say what the doc is based on (Council discussion, Ms Wendy's v2) and list the points needing feedback.
- If Gail disagrees with a partner or client proposal, lay it out neutrally: what was agreed, what's proposed now, the risk to OA, and a suggested middle ground.
