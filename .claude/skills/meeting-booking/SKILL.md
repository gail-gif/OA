# Meeting booking and time zones

Read the `oa-basics` skill first. Gail's calendar is in Asia/Manila (PHT).

## Converting times
- Always show the client's time **and** PHT, with the day, e.g. "Wed 3pm PST = Thu 7am PHT".
- Mention daylight saving: US clocks change early November; Sydney/Melbourne are on daylight saving Oct–Apr; WA (Perth) = PHT all year.
- Double-check the date when a conversion crosses midnight.

## Creating an event
- Title: "Call with <Name>", "Freedom Audit – <Name>", "Freedom Plan Presentation – <Company>".
- Add a Google Meet link and invite the client plus anyone Gail names. If an invite is blocked by the permission prompt, tell her who still needs inviting.
- Check for duplicates and remove the old event when rescheduling.

## Reminder events
"Remind me to reach out to X in December" means a short event in Gail's calendar ("Reach out to Kirsty", "Get back to Sharon") on the date she gives, or the first working day of that week.

## Reply email
Use `email-reply`: confirm the time in both zones, include the Meet link, thank them.
For reschedules, offer 1–2 concrete options. If Gail is on leave, give the reason she gives.
