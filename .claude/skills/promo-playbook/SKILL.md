# Sales promotions playbook

Read the `oa-basics` skill first.

## Rules Gail set
- Write it **as a sales pitch** (pitch → objection handling → close), not marketing copy.
- Avoid blanket 10% discounts. Prefer value offers: books (EA Playbook, Ms Linh's "360 Degree Success", 40 Productivity Hacks eBook).
- **Discounts never stack**: one offer per client.
- Discounts apply to the **management fee only**. The Angel's rate is always paid in full.
- Early Bird (2026 example): $100 off the management fee **every month for as long as they stay**, for sign-ups before a Friday deadline.
- Deadlines fall on Fridays and fit around the office closure (about 21 Dec – 4 Jan) and 1–2 weeks of recruitment, e.g. "confirm by Fri 4 Dec = Angel before Christmas; by Fri 18 Dec = ready mid-January".
- Target the real pipeline: proposals sent, qualified leads waiting on a deposit, warm leads (by name).
- No tables if she asks. Keep it simple.

## Email to Marketing
- To Nadine, CC Ms Linh and Ms Wendy (and Charyl if recruitment capacity matters).
- Opening Gail used: "As discussed I am forwarding some ideas for the marketing for end of year start of year promotions".
- End with a "to confirm" list, each item addressed to the right person.
- When Nadine replies, explain what she means in plain words, then draft Gail's reply in the same thread.
- To edit a draft, create a new one (keeps her signature) and delete the old one.
