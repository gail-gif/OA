## Talent rates (paid to the Angel, no cut taken)

- Typical: $10–$15/hr. Specialists such as creative, marketing or paid ads: $12–$15/hr or more.
- Use the range Gail quoted on the call.

## Hours and totals

- Minimum: **20 hours a week** (4 hours a day, Monday to Friday). Hours can go up gradually, e.g. 25 or 30 for busy periods.
- Monthly hours: 20 hrs/week = **86.67 hrs/month**; 40 hrs/week = **173.3 hrs/month**.
- Estimated total = OA fee + (monthly hours × hourly rate). Round to the nearest $10.
  - Example: Essential $720 + 86.67 × $12–$15 = **$1,760–$2,020**
  - Example: Managed USD $1,000 + 86.67 × $12–$15 = **$2,040–$2,300**
- If Gail's figure on the call differs from this math, point it out to her before she sends anything.

## Terms

- **$500 Freedom Plan deposit** starts recruitment. Refunded if the client changes direction before interviewing, or at the end of the contract with no outstanding balance. There's nothing to sign at deposit stage.
- **3-month minimum**, then month to month with **30 days' notice**.
- **Recruitment:** 1–2 weeks (sooner if there's a match in the pool). The client sees 3–4 profiles with resume, portfolio or work samples, intro video, hourly rate, tools, personality assessment, computer specs and internet speed. Optional skills or creativity test before interview; paid trial possible.
- **Vetting:** top 1%, 3+ years' experience, panel interviews, role-specific testing, character referencing. "You only teach your process, not the skills."
- **Time zone:** Angels work the client's business hours, including AU and US hours.
- **Reliability:** Angels have backup internet. They send end-of-day reports with hours per task. The client adds them to WhatsApp, Slack or Google Chat.

## Contractor status and leave (Gail's exact position)

- Angels are **contracted to Outsourcing Angel** and paid **by the hour as contractors**. Everything sits under the agency.
- **Australian employment requirements do not apply:** no superannuation, no leave accrual, no employee entitlements.
- **Leave:** OA has no leave policy. The client decides whether to give paid leave. Otherwise absences, planned leave and public holidays are made up by the Angel or are unpaid.
